§01 · Paper II + Appendix A/B · C1 C2 C3 C6

The Physical Scale 物理规模

A $41m/MW campus is a chip stack, not a building: two-thirds of the cost is IT equipment, and the whole buildout rests on a 494 GW pipeline of which the largest slice has no completion date at all. 每 MW 4,100 万美元的校区，本质是芯片堆而非盖房子：成本约三分之二是 IT 设备；整个建设计划压在一条 494 GW 的管道上——其中最大的一块根本没有完工日期。

The 200 MW campus costs $8.216B in total: $2.2B for the facility (200 MW × $11m, close to JLL's $11.3m/MW), $0.4B of power infrastructure, and $5.616B of IT equipment — 1,170 GB300 NVL72 racks at about $4.8m installed. The paper's own benchmarks flag the power line as "likely conservative" against Moody's implied $2.44m/MW, and liquid cooling adds 7–10% (Turner & Townsend). 200 MW 校区合计 82.16 亿美元：设施 22 亿（200 MW × 每兆瓦 1,100 万美元，接近 JLL 的每兆瓦 1,130 万美元）、电力基础设施 4 亿、IT 设备 56.16 亿——约 1,170 个 GB300 NVL72 机架、装机单价约 480 万美元。论文自己的基准比较提示：相对 Moody's 隐含的每兆瓦 244 万美元，电力项「很可能偏保守」，液冷另加 7–10%（Turner & Townsend）。

Caveat · 口径
The pipeline is "not a completion forecast: projects may be delayed, reduced in scale, or never completed"; the 188.3/114.9/213.5 GW split is "a scenario rather than a forecast". Imputation caps at 2,000 MW per project; 367 projects without reported capacity add 118.2 GW (21.2%, derived) of model-estimated capacity. 管道「不是完工预测：项目可能推迟、缩规模或永不完工」；188.3/114.9/213.5 GW 的三分是「情景而非预测」。缺失容量的插补以单项目 2,000 MW 封顶；367 个缺容量项目由模型补出 118.2 GW（21.2%，衍生计算）。
§02 · Paper II (tail) + Appendix C · C4 C5 C24

Investment & the Historical Ledger 投资与历史对比

$8.973 trillion over 2025–32 — front-loaded to a $1,717B peak in 2028, a −43% cliff in 2029 — averaging 3.22% of GDP per year: above every prior U.S. capex boom's average, 1.44× the railroads. 2025–32 年合计 8.973 万亿美元——前倾至 2028 年 1.717 万亿美元峰值、2029 年骤降 43%——年均占 GDP 3.22%：高于美国历史上任何一轮资本开支热潮的平均值，为铁路的 1.44 倍。

Epistemics · 认识论不对称
Five rows are measured, one is modeled: the five historical episodes are ex-post measurements from heterogeneous sources (Cranmer 1960 for canals; Gallman's 1860-dollar railroad series excluding rolling stock), while the AI row is a scenario. Asset lives differ too — canals, railroads and fiber served for decades, GPUs may obsolesce in three to six years, so gross-investment comparisons overstate net capital formation. 五行是测量、一行是模型：五轮历史热潮是来自异质来源的事后测量（运河用 Cranmer 1960；铁路用 Gallman 的 1860 美元序列、不含机车车辆），而 AI 行是情景。资产寿命也不同——运河、铁路与光纤服务数十年，GPU 可能三到六年内过时，故总投资口径的比较会高估净资本形成。
§03 · Paper III · C7 C8 C9

The Financing Turn 融资转向

In 2026 the five hyperscalers' capex ($800.5B) overtakes their combined operating cash flow ($707.1B) for the first time — and the marginal dollar is financed outside banks and mostly off the big five's balance sheets. 2026 年，五家超大规模厂商的资本开支（$800.5B）首次超过其合计经营现金流（$707.1B）——新增的每一美元，都在银行体系之外、且大部分在五家超大规模厂商的资产负债表之外融通。

Caveat · 口径
2026 values mix reported results, guidance and Wall Street estimates; hyperscaler capex includes non-AI capex but excludes data centers the hyperscalers lease — the off-balance-sheet dark side continues in §05. The aggregate 60–40 split "can understate leverage at the asset level". 2026 年数值混合了已报业绩、指引与华尔街估计；超大规模厂商的资本开支含非 AI 开支、但不含租入的数据中心——表外暗部延续到 §05。总量 60–40 口径「会低估资产层面的杠杆」。
§04 · Paper IV · C10 C11 C12 C16 C23

Anatomy of Hyperion Hyperion 解剖

One deal, two leverage worlds: 90% project leverage with a 1.12× DSCR inside the JV, while Meta's own books show 25% (4% on market equity) and $130B of OCF covering 2026 debt service ≥18×. 一笔交易，两个杠杆世界：JV 内项目杠杆 90%、DSCR 仅 1.12×；而 Meta 自身账面杠杆 25%（按市值计仅 4%）、$130B 经营现金流对 2026 年偿债的覆盖至少 18 倍。

Template · 模板效应（C23）
"The Hyperion transaction may therefore provide a template for future data center financings." It already has: the 960 MW Sopaipilla project in El Paso, TX — a Meta–BlackRock JV — issued $12.3B of A+-rated bonds in July 2026 on a nearly identical structure. 「Hyperion 交易因此可能为未来的数据中心融资提供模板。」模板已经复制：德州 El Paso 的 960 MW Sopaipilla 项目（Meta 与 BlackRock 合资）于 2026 年 7 月以几乎相同的结构发行了 $12.3B 的 A+ 债。
§05 · Paper IV fn5–6 · C13 C14 C15

Off-Balance-Sheet Leverage 表外杠杆

Only one-fifth of the ~$3T in data-center obligations is on balance sheet; capitalizing uncommenced leases (×0.75 PV) multiplies leverage ×2.4 on a debt-to-capital basis and ×3.7 on debt/EBITDA. 约 $3T 的数据中心义务中仅五分之一在表内；把未生效租约资本化（×0.75 现值）后，债务/资本口径杠杆 ×2.4、债务/EBITDA 口径 ×3.7。

GAAP · 口径
Under current GAAP, Meta recognizes neither the future lease obligations nor the residual value guarantee before effectiveness — "even though one of the two commitments will ultimately materialize for sure". The paper's L2/L4 add lease obligations only; the $1.52T of purchase commitments enters no leverage ratio (see §09 risk-2). 按现行 GAAP，Meta 在生效前对未来租约与残值担保均不予确认——「尽管两项承诺中必有一项最终兑现」。论文的 L2/L4 只加回租赁义务；$1.52T 采购承诺不进入任何杠杆比率（见 §09 risk-2）。
§06 · Paper V (front) · C20 C21 C22

Risks: the Market Has Already Voted 风险：市场已经投票

All five hyperscaler CDS spreads widened (Oracle +73% to 205.5bp, derived from endpoints), data-center REIT betas doubled from ~0.5 to ~1, and NVIDIA backstops its own chip demand with up to $125B. 五家超大规模厂商 CDS 利差全员走阔（Oracle +73% 至 205.5bp，由端点衍生计算）、数据中心 REIT beta 从约 0.5 翻倍至约 1、NVIDIA 至多以 $125B 为自家芯片需求兜底。

Sample note · 样本口径
CDS values are two endpoints of an observation window, not a path. Amazon — excluded from footnote 6's L1–L4 leverage recompute because its leases include many warehouses — was the second-hardest repriced (+50.1%, derived); the ratio-based and market-based evidence cover different firm sets. CDS 数值是观察窗口的两个端点而非路径。Amazon 因租约含大量仓库而被脚注 6 的 L1–L4 杠杆重算剔除，其重定价却是第二大的（+50.1%，衍生计算）；比率型证据与市场型证据覆盖的公司集合不同。
§07 · Paper V (tail) + Appendix D · C17 C18 C19

The Revenue Requirement 收入要求

The capital ladder is fully reproducible: $8.762T cost → ×1.04527 → $9.159T base → ×19.37% → $1.774T cash flow → ÷50% margin → $3.549T required revenue — 8.8% of 2032 GDP, ≈$5.1/GPU-hr at full utilization against the $6.07 spot rent. 资本阶梯完全可复算：$8.762T 成本 → ×1.04527 → $9.159T 基座 → ×19.37% → $1.774T 现金流 → ÷50% 利润率 → $3.549T 所需年收入——占 2032 年 GDP 的 8.8%，满负荷约 $5.1/GPU 时，对照 $6.07 的现货租金。

Scope · 声明
"The exercise is not a forecast of AI revenues. Rather, it calculates the mature annual revenue required to recover the investment, replace depreciating capital, and compensate investors for the opportunity cost of capital." OpenAI+Anthropic arithmetic (fixed text strip in the lab): $100B in 2026 × 1.813^6 ≈ $3.54T — 81.3%/yr through 2032. 「这不是对 AI 收入的预测。它计算的是为收回投资、更换折旧资本并补偿投资者的资本机会成本所需的成熟期年收入。」OpenAI+Anthropic 算术（实验台底部固定文字条）：2026 年 $100B × 1.813^6 ≈ $3.54T——至 2032 年年增 81.3%。
§08 · Paper VI · Policy Priorities

Policy Priorities 政策优先级

Four priorities, in the paper's order: measurement, disclosure & accounting, valuation & capital structure, and financial stability. 按论文顺序的四个优先级：度量、披露与会计、估值与资本结构、金融稳定。

① Measurement · 度量
Who bears demand, refinancing, and residual-value risk as the buildout migrates off hyperscaler balance sheets? Better measurement comes first. 随着建设迁出超大规模厂商的资产负债表，谁承担需求、再融资与残值风险？改进度量居首。
② Disclosure & Accounting · 披露与会计
Project debt, JV equity, future leases, residual-value guarantees, and tenant concentration — the items GAAP defers recognizing before effectiveness. 项目债、合资权益、未来租约、残值担保与租户集中度——即 GAAP 在生效前推迟确认的那些项目。
③ Valuation & Capital Structure · 估值与资本结构
The mismatch between short-lived IT (3–6 years to obsolescence) and long-lived buildings and power (20 years) sits at the center of the required-revenue math. 短寿命 IT（3–6 年即过时）与长寿命建筑和电力（20 年）的错配，正是收入要求算术的核心。
④ Financial Stability · 金融稳定
Off-balance-sheet structures make the relevant exposures harder to observe before a downturn — opacity priced already in CDS (§06) and concentrated in undated circular financing. 表外结构让相关敞口在下行来临前更难被观察到——不透明性已在 CDS 中被定价（§06），并集中于无日期的环状融资。
